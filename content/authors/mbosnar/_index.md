**Mihovil Bosnar** was an assistant at the Ruđer Bošković Institute's Division of Theoretical Physics from 2017 to 2021, where he conducted his doctoral research in condensed matter physics under the supervision of [Ivor Lončarić](/author/ivor-loncaric/). He defended his dissertation, [_Electronic Properties of van der Waals Heterostructures from First Principles_](https://fulir.irb.hr/7462/), in 2021. He applied density-functional calculations to two-dimensional crystal bilayers, PtSe<sub>2</sub> on platinum surfaces, and electrically controllable magnetization in graphene.

His work led to publications including [Proximity-induced magnetization in graphene: towards efficient spin gating](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.4.114006) and research on selenium intercalation at PtSe<sub>2</sub>/Pt interfaces. Alongside his research, he helped teach introductory and advanced physics laboratory courses at the Faculty of Science, presented his work at eight scientific meetings, and completed a research visit to Forschungszentrum Jülich in November 2019.

*Mihovil was a research assistant in the Modelling and Theory of Materials Group from 2017 to 2021.*
