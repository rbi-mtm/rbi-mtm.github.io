**Fran Dabo, MSc** is a PhD student in theoretical condensed matter physics.

He completed his master’s thesis in Physics under the mentorship of Dr. Ryan Requist, titled *Dynamics of energy transfer between electrons and nuclei in model condensed matter systems*. For his PhD, supervised by Dr. Ryan Requist, he is continuing his research into transfer phenomena in condensed matter systems using tensor networks.

Fran has been a PhD student in the Modelling and Theory of Materials Group since 2026.
