## Multifunctional nanocarriers for nonlinear microscopy: new tools for biology and medicine

Multiphoton microscopy enables three-dimensional visualisation of biological tissues, helping researchers understand the relationship between their structure and function. Micro4Nano develops methods for imaging to a depth of up to 1 mm, combining hyperspectral imaging, second-harmonic generation, fluorescent labels and Raman microscopy.

The project also develops multifunctional nanocarriers for two-photon microscopy and drug delivery, with the aim of monitoring living cells and tissues in real time. Its network training programme supports early-career researchers and interdisciplinary collaboration.
